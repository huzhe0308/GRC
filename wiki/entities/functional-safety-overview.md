# Functional Safety Overview 2026 Q2

## Overview

This is an internal quarterly overview document (dated 13 July 2026) from the ICV Functional Safety working group, prepared by Zhang, Liuyang (R-VKO). It maps the landscape of upcoming FuSa/SOTIF technical standards in China, showing the status of each standard across pre-study, pre-plan, working list, standard list, and forecast phases. The document serves as a planning basis for development premises, cycle plans, and investment/resource decisions, tracking approximately 20+ standards in various stages of development from published to pre-study. The document is heavily image-based (slide deck format) with status tracking for standards replacing GB/T 44721, GB/T 44461, and new STPA, BMS FuSa, braking FuSa, and CDA (Combined Driver Assistance) standards.

## Technical Details

- Standards tracked across 5 phases: Pre-study, Pre-plan, Working List, Standard List, Forecast
- Key replacements: GB/T 39086-2020 (BMS FuSa), GB/T 43254-2023 (drive motor FuSa)
- New reference standards cited: ISO 19725:2026 (steer-by-wire), ISO PAS 8800:2024 (AI safety)
- Supporting standards relationships: GB 47955 (L2 CDA), GB 44721 (ADS), GB 41630 (L2 parking)
- Public comment phase targeted for 07/2026 for braking FuSa Part 1
