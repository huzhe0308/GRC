# GB/T 41798-2022 智能网联汽车自动驾驶功能场地试验方法及要求

## Overview

GB/T 41798-2022 specifies track (closed-course) testing methods and requirements for automated driving functions of intelligent and connected vehicles (智能网联汽车自动驾驶功能场地试验方法及要求). Published on 12 October 2022 with effective date 1 May 2023, it defines how to evaluate automated driving systems (ADS) on controlled test tracks, covering test site/environment requirements, test equipment/data collection, test vehicle requirements, test pass criteria, and seven categories of test methods. This standard serves as the physical testing counterpart to GB/T 47025-2026 (simulation testing) and is referenced by GB/T 44850-2024 (ICV driving safety testing).

## Scope and Requirements

- **Scope**: Specifies track testing methods and requirements for automated driving functions of ICVs
- **Clause 4 (General Requirements)**: 
  - 4.1 Test site and test environment requirements
  - 4.2 Test equipment and data collection requirements
  - 4.3 Test vehicle requirements
- **Clause 5 (Test Process and Pass Criteria)**:
  - 5.1 Process management
  - 5.2 Test pass/fail conditions
- **Clause 6 (Test Methods)** — Seven test categories:
  - 6.1 Traffic signal recognition and response (交通信号识别及响应)
  - 6.2 Road traffic infrastructure and obstacle recognition and response (道路交通基础设施与障碍物识别及响应)
  - 6.3 Pedestrian and non-motorized vehicle recognition and response (行人与非机动车识别及响应)
  - 6.4 Surrounding vehicle driving state recognition and response (周边车辆行驶状态识别及响应)
  - 6.5 Automatic emergency avoidance (自动紧急避险)
  - 6.6 Parking (停车)
  - 6.7 Dynamic driving task intervention and takeover (动态驾驶任务干预及接管)

## Technical Details

- Published: 2022-10-12; Effective: 2023-05-01
- Seven distinct test method categories covering the full ADS evaluation scope
- Test process includes both management and pass/fail criteria
- Referenced by GB/T 47025-2026 (simulation testing) as a normative reference
- Complements GB/T 44850-2024 which provides broader ICV driving safety test items
