# GB 18384-2025 电动汽车安全要求

## Overview

GB 18384-2025 is a Chinese mandatory national standard specifying safety requirements for electric vehicles (电动汽车安全要求), replacing GB 18384-2020. It is a core component of China's NEV (New Energy Vehicle) regulatory framework, covering electrical safety, rechargeable electrical energy storage system (REESS) safety, and post-crash safety requirements for battery electric vehicles (BEV) and hybrid electric vehicles (HEV/PHEV). The standard aligns with international EV safety frameworks including UN R100 and GTR 20. The source document is image-only (binary PDF format, 43 pages), which limits detailed text extraction.

## Scope and Requirements

> **Note:** The source file for GB 18384-2025 is an image-only PDF (43 pages). Detailed textual content could not be extracted from the binary file. The following is based on the standard's position within China's EV safety regulatory framework and its relationship to the superseded GB 18384-2020 and related standards.

- **Electrical Safety:** Covers electrical isolation requirements between high-voltage bus and chassis, protection against electric shock, insulation resistance thresholds for AC and DC high-voltage buses.
- **REESS Safety:** Requirements for rechargeable electrical energy storage systems including battery thermal management, overcharge/over-discharge protection, over-temperature protection, and short-circuit protection.
- **Post-Crash Safety:** Requirements for electrical safety after vehicle crash, including REESS retention, electrolyte leakage limits, isolation resistance maintenance, and fire resistance.
- **Charging Safety:** Requirements for charging interface safety, including overcurrent protection, short-circuit protection, and charging interlock mechanisms.
- **High-Voltage Marking:** Requirements for high-voltage warning labels and markings on REESS and high-voltage components.
- **Water Immersion:** Requirements for electrical safety after water immersion conditions.
