# GB/T 47001-2025 智能网联汽车数字身份及认证通用规范

## Overview

GB/T 47001-2025 specifies general specifications for digital identity and authentication of intelligent and connected vehicles (智能网联汽车数字身份及认证通用规范). The standard defines digital identity encoding, carrier requirements, and authentication mechanisms using Chinese national cryptographic algorithms (SM2/SM3/SM4), along with corresponding test methods. Proposed by the Ministry of Public Security, it provides the foundation for secure vehicle identity verification in vehicle-to-everything (V2X) communication scenarios, supporting three certificate types for different authentication contexts.

## Scope and Requirements

- **Scope** (Ch.1): Specifies requirements for ICV digital identity encoding, carriers, and identity authentication; describes corresponding test methods
- **Applicability**: Applies to ICV digital identity authentication process design and carrier development and testing
- **Digital Identity** (Ch.5): Defined as a combination of numbers and letters linking vehicle attribute information and uniquely identifying a vehicle in V2X application environments; encoding format specified in Appendix A
- **Identity Carriers** (Ch.6): Specifies requirements for digital identity carriers (chips/modules) that store digital identity, keys, digital certificates, and vehicle attribute information with authorized access mechanisms and cryptographic functions
  - 6.1 Carriers without external power supply (无外部供电)
  - 6.2 Carriers with external power supply (有外部供电)
- **Authentication** (Ch.7):
  - 7.1 General requirements for authentication mechanisms
  - 7.2 Symmetric cryptographic algorithm-based authentication (基于对称加密算法机制)
  - 7.3 Digital signature-based authentication (基于数字签名技术机制) per GB/T 15843.3-2023
- **Certificate Types**: Three certificate types defined:
  - Registration Identity Certificate (登记身份证书) — format in Appendix B
  - V2X Enrolment Certificate (V2X注册证书)
  - V2X Pseudonym Certificate (V2X假名证书)
- **Authentication Flows** (Appendix C): Three authentication processes for each certificate type
- **Test Methods** (Ch.8): Test conditions, test configuration, carrier tests for both powered and unpowered types

## Technical Details

- Cryptographic algorithms: SM2 (elliptic curve public key, GB/T 32918.2), SM3 (hash algorithm, GB/T 32905), SM4 (block cipher, GB/T 32907)
- Randomness testing: GB/T 32915 (binary sequence randomness)
- Entity authentication: per GB/T 15843.3-2023 (digital signature techniques)
- Electronic vehicle identification: aligned with GB/T 35788 (security requirements) and GB/T 35789.1 (general specifications)
- LTE-V2X security: references GB/T 45112-2024 for certificate management system
- VIN integration: GB 16735 (vehicle identification number) used as vehicle attribute in digital identity
