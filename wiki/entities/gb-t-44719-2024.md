# GB/T 44719-2024 智能网联汽车自动驾驶功能道路试验方法及要求

## Overview

GB/T 44719-2024 specifies road test methods and requirements for automated driving functions in intelligent and connected vehicles (智能网联汽车 自动驾驶功能道路试验方法及要求). The standard leverages the inherent unpredictability of real-world traffic — where other road users' behavior and trajectory are uncontrollable and scenarios appear randomly — to validate autonomous driving system safety. It forms one pillar of China's multi-pillar testing approach alongside simulation testing (GB/T 41798) and closed-field testing, jointly constituting a complete standard system for automated driving function verification.

## Scope and Requirements

- **Scope**: Specifies road test conditions and requirements for automated driving functions, describing test methods. Applies to M and N category vehicles with automated driving functions.
- **Test Vehicle Requirements** (§4.2): M1 vehicles tested at curb mass plus personnel/equipment (not exceeding max gross mass); M2/M3 city buses at 65% laden mass; other commercial vehicles at full load. No software/hardware changes during testing.
- **Test Equipment** (§4.3): Motion data acquisition frequency ≥50 Hz; video resolution ≥1920×1080 pixels at ≥30 fps; speed accuracy ≤0.1 km/h; distance accuracy ≤0.1 m; acceleration accuracy ≤0.1 m/s². Equipment must not interfere with ADS operation.
- **Test Duration** (§4.4): Total effective test duration ≥72 hours. If ADS active in both day and night: ≥48h day + ≥24h night. If active only in day or night: test only during active period. Night test required if function active below 50 lx.
- **Time Period Coverage** (Table 1): Single continuous tests must cover congestion periods (td1/tn1) and free-flow periods (td2/tn2) for the applicable time of day.
- **Function Activation** (§5.2.1): After each ignition cycle, ADS must be either inactive or in ready state (unless auto start-stop). Must have dedicated activation control; activation only when conditions met.
- **Dynamic Driving Task** (§5.2.2): No accidents attributable to VUT during active ADS; must not cross lane markings (except lane changes); stop behind stop lines; obey speed limits; avoid emergency lanes; yield at pedestrian crossings; follow traffic signals and signs.
- **Fallback Readiness** (§5.2.3): ADS must identify planned events causing intervention requests; if intervention request issued, VUT must continue DDT and maintain request signal until takeover or MRC execution.
- **Status Indication** (§5.2.4): Optical signals for ready/active states; distinct signals for state transitions, failures, intervention requests, and minimum risk strategy execution.
- **Road Environment Types**: Type I (高速公路/快速路), Type II (城市主干路/次干路/支路), Type III (other road environments).

## Technical Details

- **Road Geometry Requirements** (Appendix A): Type I requires curves with radius 400-650m; Type II requires curves with radius 60-400m; Type III requires curves ≤60m. Grade slopes 3%-6% for all types, >6% for Type III only.
- **Test Personnel**: Test operation staff (执行动态驾驶任务/接管) and on-board test staff (记录试验数据和事件). Staff must not cause intervention requests through personal behavior.
