# GB/T 47031-2026 智能网联汽车自动泊车系统性能要求与试验方法

## Overview

GB/T 47031-2026 specifies performance requirements and test methods for automated parking systems in intelligent and connected vehicles (智能网联汽车 自动泊车系统性能要求与试验方法). The standard defines two system classes (Ⅰ and Ⅱ), covering parking-in/parking-out functionality, cruise driving in parking lots, remote braking, obstacle avoidance, and exception handling. It is a comprehensive standard that integrates functional safety (per GB/T 44721-2024 Appendix A), cybersecurity (per GB 44495), and data recording (per GB 44497) requirements, making it one of the most practically complete ICV performance standards in China's regulatory framework.

## Scope and Requirements

- **Scope**: Specifies general requirements, performance requirements, and test methods for automated parking systems. Covers parking in parking lots and on-street parking spaces.
- **System Classification** (§4.1.1):
  - **Type Ⅰ**: Must have automated parking-in; may have parking-out. Does not require cruise, remote braking, or obstacle avoidance during cruise.
  - **Type Ⅱ**: Must have parking-in and cruise; may have parking-out. Must meet ALL requirements including cruise, obstacle avoidance, and remote braking.
- **Supported Parking Space Types** (Table 1): Marking-line spaces (perpendicular, parallel, angled) — all mandatory; spatial spaces (perpendicular, parallel, angled) — optional; mechanical spaces — optional.
- **Functional Requirements** (§4.2):
  - System must comply with GB/T 44721-2024 Chapters 4-6 (general, functional, HMI requirements)
  - Parking speed ≤10 km/h (§4.2.1.4h)
  - Type Ⅱ cruise speed ≤30 km/h (§4.2.1.5a)
  - Must detect obstacles including traffic cones, parking locks, 4-wheel vehicles, 2-wheel vehicles, pedestrians
  - After parking-in: shift to P, apply parking brake; if unoccupied: shut down power, lock doors
  - Remote braking required for unoccupied operation
- **Functional Safety** (§4.3): Must comply with GB/T 44721-2024 Appendix A
- **Cybersecurity** (§4.4): Must comply with GB 44495
- **Data Recording** (§4.5): Must comply with GB 44497
- **State Transitions** (§4.2.4): Defines transitions between not-ready → ready → active → remote braking / MRC states, with conditions for each transition.

## Technical Details

- **Parking Time** (§5.1.1): Type Ⅰ parking-in and parking-out each ≤50s; Type Ⅱ parking-in ≤50s.
- **Parking Position Accuracy** (§5.1.3):
  - Spatial parallel (no curb): Df/Dr ±0.15m, angle α ±3°
  - Spatial parallel (with curb): Df/Dr 0.05-0.35m, angle ±3°
  - Spatial perpendicular: Df/Dr 0.30-0.90m, angle ±3°, Mf ±0.40m
  - Marking-line spaces: all clearances >0m, angle ±3°
- **Parking-Out Position** (§5.1.4): Angle ±5°, Df/Dr 0.50-1.50m for all space types.
- **Cruise Lane Keeping** (§5.2.1): Lateral deviation range h ≤0.6m; no tire crossing lane boundary.
- **Speed Bump** (§5.2.2): If design speed >15 km/h, must detect and pass at ≤15 km/h.
- **Remote Braking Time** (Table 2): v≤10 km/h → T≤3.0s; 10<v≤20 → T≤4.0s; 20<v≤30 → T≤5.0s.
- **Slope Operation** (§5.4.6.3): Must stop and start on slopes ≥8% grade without rollback.
- **Test Equipment** (§6.1.3): Speed accuracy outdoor ≤0.1 km/h, indoor ≤0.3 km/h; position accuracy dynamic ≤0.10m, static ≤0.01m; sampling ≥50 Hz; acceleration accuracy ≤0.10 m/s²; video ≥1920×1080 at ≥30 fps.
- **Test Environment** (§6.2): Temperature -20°C to 45°C; indoor illumination ≥30 lx; outdoor wind ≤7.9 m/s, illumination ≥500 lx, visibility ≥100m, no precipitation.
- **Test Methods** (§7): Closed test site tests, public parking lot tests, and simulation tests.
- **Parking Space Dimensions**: Vertical space 2.5m × 6.0m; for vehicles >1.9m wide, Y = vehicle width + 1.0m.
- **Traffic Lane Width**: Parallel ≥3.8m; perpendicular 5.5-9.0m; angled 3.8-4.5m.
