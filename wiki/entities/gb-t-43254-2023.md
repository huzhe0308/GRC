# GB/T 43254-2023 电动汽车用驱动电机系统功能安全要求及试验方法

## Overview

GB/T 43254-2023 specifies functional safety requirements and testing methods for electric vehicle drive motor systems (电动汽车用驱动电机系统功能安全要求及试验方法). The standard covers item definition, hazard analysis and risk assessment (HARA), functional safety requirements for drive motor torque control, and verification/validation activities. It defines seven hazard categories for drive motor torque behavior, each with associated safety goals, ASIL ratings, safe states, and fault tolerant time intervals (FTTI). The standard includes informative appendices with HARA examples and FTTI determination methods, providing a practical implementation framework aligned with GB/T 34590 (ISO 26262).

## Scope and Requirements

- **Scope**: Specifies functional safety requirements and testing methods for EV drive motor systems (驱动电机系统), including drive motor, motor controller, and auxiliary devices
- **Item Definition** (Ch.5): Defines drive motor system as the relevant item per GB/T 34590.3-2022, including functional concept (drive torque output, braking torque output) and operating conditions/environmental constraints (temperature, humidity, road conditions, drive/brake/standby modes)
- **HARA** (Ch.6): Requires hazard analysis and risk assessment per GB/T 34590.3-2022, deriving safety goals and ASIL ratings
- **Functional Safety Requirements** (Ch.7) — seven key hazard categories with safety goals:

| # | Safety Goal | ASIL | Safe State | FTTI Reference |
|---|------------|------|------------|----------------|
| 7.1 | Prevent motor inability to output drive torque | A | Issue warning | 7.1.3 |
| 7.2 | Prevent excessive unexpected drive torque | C | Issue warning, terminate torque | 7.2.3 |
| 7.3 | Prevent reversed torque output direction | C | Issue warning, terminate torque | 7.3.3 |
| 7.4 | Prevent unexpected drive torque output | C | Issue warning, terminate torque | 7.4 |
| 7.5 | Prevent motor inability to output braking torque | A | Issue warning | 7.5 |
| 7.6 | Prevent excessive unexpected braking torque | C | Issue warning, terminate torque | 7.6 |
| 7.7 | Prevent unexpected braking torque | C | Issue warning, terminate torque | 7.7 |

- **Verification and Validation** (Ch.8): General requirements, functional safety verification, and functional safety confirmation activities
- **Appendix A (Informative)**: HARA example with drive motor system as relevant item — item definition, vehicle-level hazard identification, scenario analysis, ASIL derivation, safety goals and safe states
- **Appendix B (Informative)**: FTTI determination method example — FTTI definition and example for excessive unexpected drive torque

## Technical Details

- Drive motor system defined per GB/T 19596-2017: includes drive motor, motor controller, and auxiliary devices (including integrated transmission)
- Motor controller: controls power transmission between power source and motor, comprising control signal interface circuit, motor control circuit, and power electronic module
- Seven hazard categories cover both drive and braking torque failure modes
- ASIL ratings: Category A (lowest) for inability to output torque; Category C for unexpected/excessive/reversed torque
- Safe state: typically "issue warning and terminate torque output" for ASIL C hazards
