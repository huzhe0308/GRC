# Notification on Automotive Cyber Security Management System Certification

## Overview

MOLIT Notification No. 2025-637 is a Korean government notification prescribing the delegated matters for Automotive Cyber Security Management System (CSMS) certification. Issued on 4 November 2025 by the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport (MOLIT), it establishes certification criteria, audit procedures (document audit and on-site audit), fee payment, suspension rules, and certificate validity/modification/renewal processes for automotive CSMS in Korea. The notification entered into force on its date of promulgation and is subject to re-review every three years starting from 1 January 2026.

## Scope and Requirements

- **Article 1 (Purpose)**: Prescribes delegated matters for CSMS certification under MVMA Articles 30-9 through 30-12 and Enforcement Regulations Articles 40-26 through 40-30; also covers cyber attacks/threats and security measures under Article 18-4 of the Motor Vehicle Regulations
- **Article 2 (Definitions)**: Defines CSMS certification, certification audit (document audit + on-site audit), document audit (written/face-to-face inquiry), and on-site audit (interviews, site checks)
- **Article 3 (Preliminary Requirements)**: Applicant must prepare documents/evidence, venue/facilities/equipment, and other items specified by the performance testing agent
- **Article 4 (Application)**: Requires submission of Automotive CSMS Specifications and documents proving compliance with certification criteria (Article 6); modification certification required for changes to certified trade name, CSMS organization, or original manufacturer trade name
- **Article 5 (Payment of Fees)**: Applicant must pay fees determined by the performance testing agent by the designated date
- **Article 6 (Certification Criteria)**: Table 1 defines 12 certification criteria categories: (1) Scope of Application, (2) Cyber Security Management Processes, (3) Risk Identification Processes, (4) Risk Treatment Processes, (5) Risk Management Verification Processes, (6) Testing Processes, (7) Risk Assessment Update Processes, (8) Monitoring Processes, (9) Data Collection Processes, (10) Processing Period, (11) Continuous Monitoring, (12) Supply Chain Management
- **Article 7 (Audit Methods)**: Certification audit conducted as document audit + on-site audit; on-site audit may be substituted with remote audit during disasters per the Framework Act on the Management of Disasters and Safety
- **Article 7(3)**: If audit results don't comply, applicant may be given up to **100 days** for supplements
- **Article 8 (Suspension)**: Audit may be suspended for: deliberate delays/interference by applicant, insufficient readiness, unmet supplement deadlines, or disasters/business environment changes
- **Article 9 (Objection)**: Applicant may submit Objection Application within **15 days** of the measure; performance testing agent must respond within **30 days**
- **Article 10 (Certificate)**: Validity period of **3 years** from date of issuance; renewal must be applied for no later than **5 months** before expiration
- **Article 12**: Performance testing agent may request operational data including cyber attack/threat response status, effectiveness of existing security measures, and supplementary measures implemented
- **Article 13 (Cyber Attacks/Threats)**: Defined in Table 3 per Article 18-4 Subparagraph 2 Item C of Motor Vehicle Regulations
- **Article 14 (Security Measures)**: Defined in Table 4 per Article 18-4 Subparagraph 4

## Technical Details

- Certificate validity: **3 years** from issuance date (Article 10(3))
- Certificate renewal deadline: **5 months** before expiration (Article 4(3))
- Supplement period for non-compliance: **up to 100 days** (Article 7(3))
- Objection application window: **15 days** from date of measure (Article 9(1))
- Objection response deadline: **30 days** (Article 9(2))
- CSMS must cover: development, production, and post-production phases (Table 1, Criterion 1)
- Re-review period: every **3 years** starting from 2026.01.01 (Article 17)
- 12 certification criteria categories established in Table 1 referencing Article 30-9 Paragraph 3 of the Act
