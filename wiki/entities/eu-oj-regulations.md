# EU Official Journal Regulations (2025)

## Overview

Two Commission Implementing Regulations published in the EU Official Journal on 5 September 2025 lay down the detailed technical rules for implementing the Euro 7 emission type-approval framework under Regulation (EU) 2024/1257. Regulation 2025/1706 covers exhaust and evaporative emission type-approval test procedures for M1 and N1 vehicles, referencing UN R154 (WLTP), UN R168 (RDE), and UN R83 (crankcase/low-temp). Regulation 2025/1707 covers on-board monitoring (OBM) systems, OBFCM devices, driver warning systems, Environmental Vehicle Passport (EVP), and anti-tampering measures. Together they establish the implementing acts for the Euro 7 framework, with application dates from 29 November 2026 for new types and 29 November 2027 for new vehicles.

## Scope and Requirements — Regulation 2025/1706

- Establishes testing procedures, methodologies, tests and checks for exhaust and evaporative emission type-approval
- Designates vehicles as 'Euro 7', 'Euro 7G', 'Euro 7ext', or 'Euro 7Gext' per Articles 4–5 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1257
- Refers to UN R154 (WLTP) for laboratory exhaust emission measurement, UN R168 (RDE) for real driving emissions, and UN R83 for crankcase gases, low ambient temperature, and in-service conformity
- Refers to UN R155 for cybersecurity measures and secure transmission of emission-related data
- Sets durability requirements reflecting average expected vehicle lifetime in the Union (Annex III of 2024/1257)
- Covers Type 1 (exhaust cold start), Type 3 (crankcase), Type 4 (evaporative), Type 5 (durability), Type 6 (low temp) tests and OBD requirements
- Prohibits manipulation devices and manipulation strategies; sets screening and assessment procedures for detecting defeat devices
- Establishes roles for manufacturers, type-approval authorities, market surveillance authorities, and recognised third parties
- Adapts emission type-approval certificate numbering system and Certificate of Conformity templates
- Small-volume manufacturer deadline: 1 July 2030
- Specifies PN10 exhaust emission requirements for RDE testing per Regulation (EU) 2024/1257

## Scope and Requirements — Regulation 2025/1707

- Covers on-board monitoring (OBM) systems including sensors and driver warning systems
- Defines OBM data, OBM trip, OBM trip data, and on-board processing requirements
- Requires harmonised inducement methods to ensure repairs without endangering road safety
- Allows manufacturers to use own cybersecurity infrastructure per UN R155 for over-the-air OBM data transmission
- Requires anonymous OBM data submission with random sampling methodology representative of in-service fleet
- Excludes vehicles identified with potential malfunction/tampering from certain in-service conformity tests
- Technology-neutral approach: certain OBM parameters may not be relevant to certain powertrains
- OBFCM (On-Board Fuel and electric energy Consumption Monitoring) device requirements
- Environmental Vehicle Passport (EVP): QR code data carrier per ISO/IEC 18004:2024
- Anti-tampering: general and technical requirements, documentation obligations
- In-vehicle display of environmental data about vehicle type and individual vehicle performance
