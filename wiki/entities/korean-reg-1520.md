# Korean Reg. 1520 — Motor Vehicle Performance and Safety Standards

## Overview

Korean Regulation No. 1520 (MOLIT Ordinance No. 1520, dated 2025-08-14) establishes the Korea Motor Vehicle Safety Standards (KMVSS) — the performance and safety standards for motor vehicles and vehicle parts. This regulation defines safety standards and self-certification standards applicable to structures and systems of motor vehicles and motorcycles, pursuant to the Motor Vehicle Management Act (MVMA). It replaces the previous Ordinance No. 1499 (2025-05-30) and covers comprehensive vehicle definitions, braking systems, steering equipment, and specialized vehicle categories. The regulation incorporates historical amendments from Ordinances 1254, 1277, 1413, and subsequent revisions through 1520.

## Scope and Requirements

- **Purpose (Article 1)**: Establishes safety standards and self-certification standards for vehicles, and standards and methods for Safety Compliance Testing of vehicles, parts, and systems
- **Vehicle Definitions**: Defines unloaded state, loaded state, axle load, wheel load, center line of vehicle, and three-dimensional reference system for vehicle measurement
- **Loaded State Calculation**: Person weight calculated as **65 kg**; 1.5 children under age 13 counted as one person; occupants seated in designated positions; luggage evenly distributed
- **Braking System Definitions (Article 25)**: Defines service brake system (主制动系统), secondary brake system (used when service brake fails), automatically controlled braking (electronic brake control system deceleration irrespective of driver input), and rotational wheel slip during braking
- **Scope**: Covers motor vehicles, motorcycles, and their parts including specialized vehicles (police, fire, ambulance, engineering rescue)
- **Applicability Notes**: Does not include disability vehicles meeting specific criteria for maximum design speed, curb weight, and dimensions
- **Historical Amendments**: Incorporates addenda from Ordinances 1254 (2023-09-22), 1277 (2023-11-23), 1413 (2024-11-29), and subsequent revisions through 1520

## Technical Details

- Person weight for loaded state: **65 kg** per person
- Children calculation: **1.5 children under age 13 = 1 person**
- Luggage distribution: evenly distributed in designated positions
- Brake system types: service brake, secondary brake, automatically controlled braking
- Previous ordinance: No. 1499 (2025-05-30)
- Historical amendment chain: 1254 → 1277 → 1413 → 1499 → 1520
