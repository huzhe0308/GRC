# GB/T 44850-2024 智能网联汽车运行安全测试项目和方法

## Overview

GB/T 44850-2024 specifies driving safety test items and methods for intelligent connected vehicles (智能网联汽车运行安全测试项目和方法). Published on 26 October 2024 with effective date 1 May 2025, the standard defines a comprehensive set of test categories covering starting/stopping, traffic signal recognition, infrastructure/obstacle recognition, pedestrian/non-motor vehicle recognition, surrounding vehicle response, intersection navigation, road segment driving, V2X cooperative driving, special zone passage, takeover, and automatic emergency avoidance. It includes 11 normative appendices detailing specific test methodologies and specifies data collection requirements including vehicle longitudinal/lateral speed, acceleration, and heading angle.

## Scope and Requirements

- **Scope**: Specifies test items and methods for ICV driving safety testing
- **Clause 4 (Test Items)**: Defines categories of safety driving ability tests including:
  - Starting/stopping (起步停车)
  - Traffic signal recognition and response
  - Road traffic infrastructure and obstacle recognition and response
  - Pedestrian and non-motor vehicle recognition and response
  - Surrounding vehicle driving state recognition and response
  - Intersection passage
  - Road segment passage
  - V2X cooperative driving capability
  - **Special zone passage**: including bus stops (公交站台), schools/hospitals (人员聚集区域), overpasses (立交桥), toll stations (收费站), exit ramps (驶出匝道), roundabouts (环形路口)
  - **Takeover (操作接管)**: manual intervention (人工干预), autonomous failure prompt takeover (自动驾驶失效提示接管), ODD exceedance prompt takeover (超出设计运行条件提示接管)
  - **Automatic emergency avoidance (自动紧急避险)**: preceding vehicle emergency braking (前车紧急制动), sudden obstacle appearance (前方突然出现障碍物), emergency vehicle avoidance (避让警车)
- **Clause 5 (Test Methods)**: General test methodology framework
- **Data Collection Requirements**: Test data must include:
  - Test time
  - Vehicle longitudinal speed (车辆纵向速度)
  - Vehicle lateral speed (车辆横向速度)
  - Vehicle longitudinal acceleration (车辆纵向加速度)
  - Vehicle lateral acceleration (车辆横向加速度)
  - Vehicle heading angle (车辆航向角)

## Technical Details

- Published: 2024-10-26; Effective: 2025-05-01
- Data collection must record: longitudinal/lateral speed, longitudinal/lateral acceleration, heading angle
- Special zone test scenarios include bus stops, schools/hospitals, overpasses, toll stations, exit ramps, and roundabouts
- Takeover test subcategories: manual intervention, autonomous failure prompt, ODD exceedance prompt
- Emergency avoidance scenarios: preceding vehicle emergency braking, sudden obstacle, emergency vehicle avoidance
- 11 normative appendices providing detailed test methodologies
