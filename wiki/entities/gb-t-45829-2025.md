# GB/T 45829-2025 乘用车转向系统功能安全要求及试验方法

## Overview

GB/T 45829-2025 specifies functional safety requirements and testing methods for passenger car steering systems, specifically targeting Electric Power Steering (EPS) systems. Published on 1 August 2025 with effective date 1 February 2026, the standard provides a systematic framework for developing functionally safe steering systems in compliance with the GB/T 34590 (ISO 26262) series. It defines safety goals for EPS systems at ASIL D level, introduces Single Element Failure Analysis (SEFA) and System Theoretic Process Analysis (STPA) as hazard analysis techniques, and specifies safety metrics including fault tolerant time intervals (FTTI) for lateral motion control.

## Scope and Requirements

- **Scope**: Specifies functional safety requirements for passenger car steering systems and describes verification and confirmation test methods
- **Applicability**: Applies to EPS systems installed on M1 category vehicles used for controlling vehicle lateral movement; other steering-related E/E systems may refer to this standard
- **General Requirements (Clause 4)**: Functional safety technology development and process requirements follow GB/T 34590.1~34590.11-2022; specific EPS requirements take precedence where specified
- **Item Definition (Clause 5)**: EPS system item definition per GB/T 34590.3-2022 section 5.4; functional requirements must satisfy GB 17675-2021; EPS must meet vehicle operating conditions and environmental constraints including abnormal power-off scenarios with backup power steering assistance
- **Hazard Analysis and Risk Assessment (Clause 6)**: Requires HARA per GB/T 34590.3-2022 section 6.4; identifies functional abnormalities and combines with operational scenarios to form hazard event lists; introduces SEFA and STPA as analysis techniques

### Safety Goals (ASIL D)
| No. | Safety Goal | ASIL | Safety Metric | FTTI | Safe State |
|-----|------------|------|---------------|------|------------|
| 1 | Vehicle unintended lateral motion (非预期侧向运动) | D | Meet safety metric for unintended lateral motion: lateral acceleration change ≤ threshold; lateral displacement ≤ threshold; yaw rate change ≤ threshold | Per Annex C method | Reduce assistance, disable assistance, or other safe state with warning |
| 2 | Vehicle unintended loss of lateral motion control (非预期失去侧向运动控制) | D | Meet safety metric: steering force/steering wheel torque ≤ threshold for loss of control | Per Annex C method | Reduce assistance, disable assistance, or other safe state with warning |

- **Normative References**: GB 17675-2021 (steering system basic requirements), GB/T 34590.1~34590.11-2022 (functional safety series)
- **Appendices**: Informative appendices for EPS item definition, HARA examples, FTTI definition (Annex C), confirmation test methods, typical safety mechanisms, and safety analysis examples

## Technical Details

- Both safety goals assigned **ASIL D** (highest safety integrity level)
- Safety metrics for Goal 1: lateral acceleration change, lateral displacement, yaw rate change — all must not exceed safety thresholds
- Safety metric for Goal 2: steering force / steering wheel torque must not exceed loss-of-control threshold
- FTTI determination method specified in Annex C
- Safe states include: reduce assistance (降助力), disable assistance (关闭助力), or other applicable safe state with driver warning
- EPS must consider abnormal power-off scenarios where backup power provides steering assistance for emergency safety
- SEFA (Single Element Failure Analysis) and STPA (System Theoretic Process Analysis) introduced as hazard analysis techniques
- Published: 2025-08-01; Effective: 2026-02-01
