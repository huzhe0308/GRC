# GB 18352.7-XXXX (C7) 轻型汽车污染物排放限值

## Overview

GB 18352.7-XXXX (China 7 / C7) is the draft next-generation Chinese national standard for light-duty vehicle emission limits and measurement methods, currently at the 技术审查稿 (technical review draft) stage. It replaces GB 18352.6-2016 (China 6) and introduces fundamental changes including tiered emission limits, greenhouse gas emission testing, non-exhaust particulate emissions (brake particles), enterprise fleet-average emission control, and a comprehensive overhaul of OBD requirements (migrating to SAE J1979-2 UDS-based protocol). The EU7 Regulation Forecast (V4.0, December 2025) provides parallel European regulatory context, while the C7 Related Standards analysis catalogs ~26 cross-referenced standards across GB, ISO, SAE, EU/UN, and GTR domains. ^[raw/papers/OBD_1_GB+18352.7-XXXX_C7_draft.md] ^[raw/articles/QA_16_C7_Related_Standards.md] ^[raw/articles/QA_12_EU7_Regulation_Forecast_V4.0.md]

## Scope and Requirements

- **Applicability**: Covers light-duty vehicles including ICE, HEV, BEV, and FCEV with max speed ≥50 km/h; extends to M1/M2/N1/N2 up to 4500 kg
- **Type I Test**: Includes tiered (分级别) emission limits with tightened values; adds greenhouse gas (GHG) emission testing
- **Type II Test (RDE)**: Revised Real Driving Emissions test procedures with tightened limits; defines RDE as emissions under actual road driving conditions
- **Type IV Test**: Adds canister bleed emissions (炭罐逸出排放) — HC emissions from canister atmospheric port due to fuel tank temperature changes, expressed as C1H2.33 equivalent
- **Evaporative Emissions**: Defines evaporative emissions including diurnal breathing emissions (C1H2.33 equivalent) and hot soak emissions (C1H2.20 equivalent); introduces EVAP system component fast-aging methodology and battery durability requirements
- **Type VI Test**: Extended temperature conditions
- **Type VIII Test (NEW)**: Non-exhaust particulate emissions (brake particles) per GTR 24
- **Enterprise Fleet-Average**: Adds fleet-average emission control requirements
- **A/C Refrigerant**: Air conditioning refrigerant usage and leakage control requirements
- **OBD Overhaul**: SAE J1979-2 (UDS) replaces classic J1979 for ICE/xHEV; SAE J1979-3 for BEV/FCV; ISO 13400-2/3/4 (DoIP) for diagnostic transport
- **Remote Data Upload (RDU)**: Requires GB/T 22239 Level III+ cybersecurity
- **Anti-tampering**: UN-R 39 odometer protection; prohibition of manipulation devices/strategies

## C7 Related Standards (~26 cross-referenced)

- **Critical new standards**: SAE J1979-2 (UDS OBD), SAE J1979-3 (ZEV OBD), SAE J1699-5 (EV OBD compliance)
- **Diagnostic transport**: ISO 13400-2/3/4 (DoIP), GB/T 42193.2 (diagnostic transport protocol)
- **Cybersecurity**: GB/T 22239 (Level III+), GB 44495-2024
- **Odometer**: UN-R 39
- **Brake emissions**: GTR 24

## Technical Details

- Canister bleed emissions expressed as **C1H2.33** equivalent; hot soak as **C1H2.20** equivalent
- Diagnostic protocol migration: SAE J1979 → SAE J1979-2 (UDS-based) for ICE/xHEV; SAE J1979-3 for BEV/FCV
- Cybersecurity requirement for RDU: GB/T 22239 **Level III+**
- EU7 new type deadline: **29 Nov 2026**; new vehicle deadline: **29 Nov 2027**
- Small-volume manufacturer deadline: **1 Jul 2030**
- ~26 cross-referenced standards across GB, ISO, SAE, EU/UN, and GTR categories
