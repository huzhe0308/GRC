# UN R131 AEBS Regulation

## Overview

UN Regulation No. 131 establishes uniform provisions for the approval of motor vehicles with regard to Advanced Emergency Braking Systems (AEBS). The regulation defines requirements for systems that automatically detect potential forward collisions with other vehicles and pedestrians, provide driver warnings, and activate vehicle braking to avoid or mitigate collision severity. The 02 series with Supplement 3 extends the scope from monotonous highway driving conditions to new scenarios including city driving. It applies to M and N category vehicles, with an alternative set of requirements available for lighter vehicles (M₁/N₁ ≤8t with hydraulic braking) under UN R152.

## Scope and Requirements

- **Scope**: Applies to approval of motor vehicles with regard to AEBS for M and N categories; extends scope to city driving scenarios (Supplement 3)
- **System Requirements**: The system shall automatically detect potential forward collisions with vehicles and pedestrians crossing the vehicle path, provide driver warning, and activate braking to decelerate the vehicle to avoid or mitigate collision severity
- **Warning**: Minimum acoustic or haptic warning (may include sharp deceleration) to alert inattentive drivers; collision warning shall be triggered at latest 0.8 seconds before emergency braking begins
- **Driver Override**: Driver can take control at any time through conscious action (e.g., steering input or accelerator kick-down) to override the system
- **Safety**: In case of system failure, safe operation of the vehicle shall not be endangered
- **False Warnings**: System shall be designed to minimize false collision warnings and false emergency braking in normal driving situations
- **Functional Safety**: Annex 4 requires functional safety documentation from the manufacturer and verification of the safety concept
- **Reciprocal Recognition**: For vehicles with M₁/N₁ ≤8t with hydraulic braking, Contracting Parties applying both R131 and R152 recognize approvals to either as equally valid
- **Sub-group Exemptions**: Certain sub-groups where AEBS benefit is uncertain or installation is technically infeasible (e.g., buses with standing passengers Classes I/II/A, Category G vehicles, construction vehicles, special purpose vehicles)
- **Conditions Acknowledgement**: Regulation acknowledges that real-world conditions (vehicle condition, road adhesion, weather, external radar interference, deteriorated infrastructure) may affect performance

## Technical Details

- Warning trigger: ≤0.8 seconds before emergency braking onset
- Collision detection: forward vehicles and pedestrians crossing vehicle path
- Override mechanisms: steering input or accelerator kick-down
- Performance conditions: tested under various vehicle speeds, road conditions, and environmental factors
- Functional safety: Annex 4 compliance required from manufacturer
