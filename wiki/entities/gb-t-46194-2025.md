# GB/T 46194-2025 智能网联汽车网络安全工程

## Overview

GB/T 46194-2025 specifies cybersecurity engineering requirements for intelligent and connected vehicles (智能网联汽车网络安全工程). The standard provides a systematic framework for managing cybersecurity throughout the vehicle lifecycle, aligned with the ISO/SAE 21434 cybersecurity engineering process. It covers threat analysis and risk assessment (TARA), cybersecurity goals and requirements derivation, cybersecurity assurance, and post-development cybersecurity management. The standard is a key component of China's connected vehicle cybersecurity regulatory ecosystem, complementing the mandatory GB 44495-2024 (vehicle cybersecurity technical requirements).

## Scope and Requirements

- **Scope**: Specifies cybersecurity engineering requirements for ICV systems including threat analysis, risk assessment, cybersecurity goals/claims, and assurance activities throughout the vehicle lifecycle.
- **Cybersecurity Lifecycle**: Covers development phase, production phase, and post-production phase cybersecurity management activities.
- **TARA Methodology**: Defines threat analysis and risk assessment procedures including asset identification, damage scenario analysis, attack path analysis, and risk evaluation.
- **Cybersecurity Goals**: Requires derivation of cybersecurity goals from TARA results, with traceability to cybersecurity requirements and claims.
- **Assurance**: Defines cybersecurity assurance levels and corresponding verification activities.
- **Relationship to GB 44495**: GB/T 46194 provides the engineering process framework; GB 44495 specifies the mandatory technical requirements that the engineering process must satisfy.
- **Relationship to UN R155**: Supports CSMS compliance by providing the engineering methodology that manufacturers must follow to demonstrate cybersecurity risk management.
