# GB 11562-2025 轻型汽车驾驶员前方视野要求及测量方法

## Overview

GB 11562-2025 is a mandatory Chinese national standard specifying requirements and measurement methods for driver forward visibility (驾驶员前方视野) in light-duty vehicles. It replaces GB 11562-2014 and introduces significant new requirements including camera-monitor system (CMS, 摄像机-监视器系统) technical requirements, field-of-vision assistant (FVA, 视野辅助系统) provisions, and extended visibility requirements for high-riding vehicles (V2 point >1650mm). The standard defines the 180° forward direct vision zone, A-pillar binocular obstruction angle limits, the "S" area evaluation zone, and measurement procedures using V-points, P-points, and E-points within a three-dimensional reference grid.

## Scope and Requirements

- **Scope** (Ch.1): Specifies requirements and measurement methods for direct visibility within 180° forward range of the driver; applies to M₁ and N₁ category vehicles
- **Windshield transparent area** (4.1): Must include at least 6 reference points — V₁ point 17° left, 7° up; V₂ point 5° down; plus symmetric mirror points on the other side
- **A-pillar binocular obstruction angle** (4.2): Each A-pillar must not exceed 6°; for armored (防弹) vehicles, max 10°
- **A-pillar count** (4.3): No more than 2 A-pillars per vehicle
- **180° forward vision zone** (4.4): Between the horizontal plane through V₁ and the three 4°-down planes through V₂, no obstructions except A-pillars, ventilation outlets, triangular window dividers, radio antennas, rearview mirrors, wipers, and provisions in 4.7–4.9
- **Camera-Monitor System (CMS)** (4.5–4.6): Obstruction exemptions apply to CMS cameras, mounts, and housings; CMS replacing exterior mirrors must not cause more obstruction than the mirrors they replace
- **Steering wheel obstruction** (4.7): Permitted if plane through V₂ tangent to steering wheel upper edge tilts ≥1° downward from horizontal
- **"S" area** (4.8): Obstruction in the zone between 1° and 4° downward planes is allowed if projection from V₂ does not exceed 20% of "S" area; FVA opaque pixels excluded if compliant with GB/T 46926-2025
- **Field-of-Vision Assistant (FVA)** (4.9): Transparent area may be covered by FVA information if FVA meets GB/T 46926-2025 Chapter 4
- **High-riding vehicles** (4.10): When V₂ point >1650mm above ground, a 1200mm-high, 300mm-diameter cylinder must be partially visible within a defined zone 2000–2300mm forward of the vehicle
- **Same-type judgment** (Ch.7): Same-type vehicles must share identical windshield shape, external components, dashboard, steering wheel position, R-point location, seat adjustment range, and design torso angle
- **Implementation timeline** (Ch.8): New type approvals from effective date; existing types +13 months; FVA requirements +13 months (new) / +25 months (existing)

## Technical Details

- V₁ point coordinates relative to R-point: X=68mm, Y=−5mm, Z=665mm (at 25° torso angle)
- V₂ point coordinates: X=68mm, Y=−5mm, Z=589mm
- P₁ point: X=35mm, Y=−20mm, Z=627mm; P₂ point: X=63mm, Y=47mm, Z=627mm
- Seat horizontal adjustment correction (ΔX): ranges from 0 (≤108mm) to −48mm (>158mm)
- E-point geometry: E₁ and E₂ are 104mm from P₁, E₁–E₂ distance = 65mm
- "S" area: located at 1500mm forward of V₂ point, bounded by 1° and 4° downward planes
- Obstruction limits for windshield antenna wires: ≤0.5mm embedded, ≤1.0mm printed; defroster wires ≤0.03mm width with ≥1.25mm spacing
- Reference grid: X-scale ≥8m, Y-scale ≥4m, minimum resolution 1mm; levelness checked every 250mm
