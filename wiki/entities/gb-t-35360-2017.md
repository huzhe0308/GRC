# GB/T 35360-2017 汽车转向系统术语和定义

## Overview

GB/T 35360-2017 specifies the terminology and definitions for motor vehicle steering systems (汽车转向系统术语和定义). This recommended national standard covers classification terms, technical parameters, characteristics, and major component terminology for all types of automotive steering systems, including mechanical, hydraulic power, electro-hydraulic power, electric power, and steer-by-wire systems. It provides the standardized vocabulary foundation used by subsequent functional safety standards for steering systems, including GB/T 45829-2025 (EPS FuSa) and GB 17675-2021 (steering system basic requirements).

## Scope and Requirements

- **Scope**: Specifies terminology and definitions for motor vehicle steering systems; applies to all types of automotive steering systems
- **Section 2 (Classification Terms)**: Defines steering system types including:
  - Mechanical steering system (MS) — fully manual operation
  - Hydraulic power steering system (HPS) — hydraulic assistance
  - Electro-hydraulic power steering system (EHPS) — hydraulic with electronic/electric control
  - Electronically controlled hydraulic power steering (ECHPS) — electronic control valve
  - Electrically powered hydraulic steering (EPHS) — motor-driven pump
  - **Electrical power steering system (EPS)** — motor-driven, with subtypes:
    - **C-EPS** (Column-type, 转向轴式): Motor and transmission fixed to steering column, drives steering shaft; includes steering sensor, motor, electronic controller
    - **P-EPS** (Pinion-type, 转向齿轮式): Motor connected to steering pinion, drives pinion gear
    - **R-EPS** (Rack-type): Motor drives rack directly
  - **Steering system by wire (SBW)** — fully electrically controlled, no mechanical linkage
- **Section 3 (Technical Parameters)**: Defines angular ratio (角传动比), transmission efficiency (传动效率), stiffness (刚度), steering wheel turns (转向盘圈数), free play (自由行程), steering torque (转向力矩), max working pressure, rated working pressure, pump displacement (排量), flow control valve parameters, and road feeling (路感)
- **Section 4 (Characteristics)**: Covers steering gear ratio characteristics, clearance characteristics, efficiency characteristics, steering force characteristics, hydraulic system response characteristics, and control valve response characteristics
- **Section 5 (Major Components)**: Defines steering control mechanism (steering wheel, transmission shaft types including energy-absorbing/吸能式, telescopic/伸缩式, ball-type/球销式), steering gears (rack and pinion/齿轮齿条式, recirculating ball/循环球式), power steering pumps, and control valves

## Technical Details

- EPS subtypes defined with specific component descriptions: C-EPS includes steering sensor (转向传感器), motor (电机), electronic controller (电子控制器)
- Key parameters: angular ratio, transmission efficiency, stiffness, free play, steering torque, max/rated working pressure, pump displacement
- Component terminology covers energy-absorbing steering columns (safety feature), telescopic shafts, and ball-type joints
- Steering gear types: rack and pinion (齿轮齿条式) and recirculating ball (循环球式)
