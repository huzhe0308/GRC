# GB/T 43758-2024 智能网联汽车运行安全测试环境技术条件

## Overview

GB/T 43758 is a multi-part standard specifying technical conditions for driving safety test environments for intelligent connected vehicles (智能网联汽车运行安全测试环境技术条件). Part 1 covers public road test environments and Part 2 covers semi-open road test environments. These standards define the infrastructure, traffic control, lighting, communication, and monitoring requirements for ICV safety testing on different road types. They form a critical component of China's multi-pillar testing framework for autonomous vehicles, alongside simulation and closed-field testing standards.

## Scope and Requirements

### Part 1: Public Road

- **Scope**: Specifies requirements for public road test environments for ICV driving safety testing, including urban roads and highways.
- **Road Environment Requirements** (Ch.4): General requirements, urban road specifications, highway specifications, surrounding environment.
- **Traffic Facility Requirements** (Ch.5): Traffic signs, markings, signals for test routes.
- **Traffic Control Requirements** (Ch.6): General, urban road, and highway traffic management.
- **Road Lighting Requirements** (Ch.7): Illumination standards for test routes.
- **Traffic Operation Requirements** (Ch.8): General, urban road, and highway traffic flow conditions.
- **Road Monitoring and Communication** (Ch.9): Monitoring/sensing infrastructure, communication networks, high-precision positioning networks.
- **Test Support Requirements** (Ch.10): Safeguards and support for testing activities.
- **Appendices**: A (public test road special signs), B (traffic congestion grading), C (safety driving ability test items).

### Part 2: Semi-Open Road

- **Scope**: Specifies requirements for semi-open road test environments for ICV driving safety testing.
- Covers semi-open road infrastructure, traffic management, safety measures, and monitoring requirements.
- Part of the multi-pillar testing framework alongside public road and closed-field testing.
- Complements GB/T 41798 (closed-field test) and GB/T 44719 (road test) standards.
