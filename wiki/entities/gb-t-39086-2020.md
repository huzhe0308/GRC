# GB/T 39086-2020 电动汽车用动力蓄电池安全要求

## Overview

GB/T 39086-2020 specifies functional safety requirements and testing methods for the battery management system (BMS) of electric vehicles (电动汽车用电池管理系统功能安全要求及试验方法). The standard aligns with the GB/T 34590 (ISO 26262 equivalent) functional safety lifecycle, adapting it specifically to the hazards unique to EV battery systems — thermal runaway, overcharge, and over-discharge. It is a critical standard for ensuring that BMS designs adequately prevent catastrophic battery failures through systematic hazard analysis and risk assessment (HARA).

## Scope and Requirements

- **Scope**: Specifies functional safety requirements and testing methods for BMS of electric vehicles, covering general requirements, item definition, HARA, functional safety requirements, and verification/validation activities.
- **General Requirements** (Chapter 4): Defines general functional safety requirements for BMS including item definition, safety lifecycle, and confirmation measures.
- **Item Definition** (Chapter 5): Defines BMS as a relevant item with functions including charging management, discharging management, thermal management, cell balancing, SOX estimation, communication, and fault diagnosis.
- **HARA** (Chapter 6): Requires hazard analysis and risk assessment per GB/T 34590 methodology, identifying vehicle-level hazards from BMS functional malfunctions.
- **Functional Safety Requirements** (Chapter 7): Addresses prevention of seven torque-related hazards: motor inability to output drive torque, excessive unexpected drive torque, reversed torque direction, unexpected drive torque output, inability to output braking torque, excessive unexpected braking torque, and unexpected braking torque.
- **Key Hazards Identified**: Charging over-voltage leading to overcharge, lithium plating, thermal runaway → fire, explosion, hazardous substance release; charging over-current causing thermal runaway; battery over-temperature during charging/discharging; discharge over-current and over-temperature; over-discharge leading to battery damage.
- **Typical Scenarios**: Normal driving (highway, urban, turning), stationary unattended charging, stationary unattended discharging, long-term parking, collision (during and after), maintenance.
- **ASIL Assessment**: Most BMS-related hazards rated ASIL C (S=3, E=4, C=2) due to thermal runaway risk with warning signs allowing escape; over-discharge scenarios rated QM/ASIL A.

## Technical Details

- **Appendix A (REESS as relevant item)**: Defines RESS boundary and interfaces, hazard identification using guide words (Loss, Incorrect, Unintended, Stuck). Example hazards include overcharge causing lithium plating and thermal runaway at voltages exceeding cell limits. ^[raw/papers/FuSa_12a_GB+T_39086-2020_附录A_REESS.md]
- **Appendix B (BMS as relevant item)**: Detailed HARA table with 7 hazard events (HZD_01 through HZD_07), covering over-charge, over-discharge, over-temperature, over-current during charge and discharge, and insulation resistance fault scenarios. Each hazard mapped to ASIL ratings with severity S=3, exposure E=4, controllability C=2 for most scenarios.
- **Appendix C**: Fault Tolerant Time Interval (FTTI) determination method example, showing how to calculate maximum time from fault occurrence to safe state transition.
- **ASIL Distribution**: Thermal runaway hazards (overcharge, over-current charge) rated ASIL C; over-discharge scenarios rated QM or ASIL A due to lower severity (battery damage vs. personal injury).
