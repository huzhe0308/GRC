# GB/T 45312-2025 智能网联汽车自动驾驶系统设计运行条件

## Overview

GB/T 45312-2025 specifies operational design conditions (ODC, 设计运行条件) for automated driving systems in intelligent connected vehicles (智能网联汽车 自动驾驶系统设计运行条件). The standard defines general requirements and a base element set for ODC, which encompasses the operational design domain (ODD), vehicle state, occupant state, and other conditions necessary for ADS operation. It provides the framework for defining the conditions under which autonomous driving systems are designed to operate, serving as a foundational reference for other ADS standards including GB/T 44721 (general technical requirements) and GB 44497 (data recording).

## Scope and Requirements

- **Scope** (Ch.1): Specifies general requirements and base element sets for ODC of automated driving systems; applies to M and N category vehicles with ADS; other vehicle types or automation levels may refer to this standard
- **Key Definitions**:
  - ADS (自动驾驶系统): Hardware and software implementing autonomous driving functions (L3+) per GB/T 40429-2021
  - ODC (设计运行条件): All conditions for ADS functional operation — includes ODD, vehicle state, occupant state, and other necessary conditions
  - ODD (设计运行范围): External environment conditions for ADS operation (roads, traffic, weather, lighting, etc.)
- **Abbreviations** (Ch.4): ADS, BDS, DDT, GALILEO, GLONASS, GPS, ODC, ODD, RTK, V2I, V2N, V2P, V2V
- **General Requirements** (Ch.5):
  - 5.1: ODC must be defined based on the ODC base element sets and requirements in this standard; additional elements may be added beyond the base set
  - ODC elements include environmental conditions (road types, traffic, weather, lighting), vehicle conditions, and other operational constraints
  - ODC definition is prerequisite for ADS type approval per GB/T 44721-2024

## Technical Details

- ODC encompasses four major categories: ODD (external environment), vehicle state, occupant state, and other necessary conditions
- ODD components: road types, traffic conditions, weather, lighting, and other external environmental factors
- Navigation references: BDS (BeiDou), GPS, GLONASS, GALILEO satellite navigation systems; RTK for precise positioning
- V2X communication: V2I (vehicle-to-infrastructure), V2N (vehicle-to-network), V2P (vehicle-to-pedestrian), V2V (vehicle-to-vehicle)
- ODC is the superordinate concept containing ODD as a subset — ODD covers external conditions while ODC also includes internal vehicle and occupant states
