# GB 18352.6-2016 (C6) 轻型汽车污染物排放限值

## Overview

GB 18352.6-2016 is the Chinese national standard specifying emission limits and measurement methods for light-duty vehicles at the China 6 (第六阶段) emission stage. It replaces GB 18352.5-2013 (China 5) and was drafted by the Chinese Research Academy of Environmental Sciences (中国环境科学研究院), Beijing Institute of Technology, China Automotive Technology and Research Center, and other institutions. The standard adopts WLTP test procedures, introduces RDE (Real Driving Emissions) testing as the Type II test, tightens emission limits, adds gasoline PM/PN measurement, and significantly revises OBD requirements (partially adopting California CCR Title 13 Section 1968.2). It is a foundational reference for the upcoming China 7 (GB 18352.7) standard.

## Scope and Requirements

- Covers spark-ignition and compression-ignition light-duty vehicles: exhaust emissions, RDE emissions, crankcase emissions, evaporative emissions, refueling emissions, durability, and OBD
- Applies to vehicles with max design speed ≥50 km/h (including HEV); production conformity and in-use conformity requirements specified
- Key changes from China 5: WLTP test cycle replaces NEDC for Type I; RDE designated as Type II test; tightened limits; gasoline PM/PN measurement added; enhanced Type VI (low temp) for diesel and NOₓ; refueling emission control; HEV test requirements added
- Test types: Type I (cold start exhaust), Type II (RDE), Type III (crankcase), Type IV (evaporative), Type V (durability), Type VI (low temp), Type VII (refueling), OBD (Appendix J)
- 18 normative annexes (A–R) covering type approval materials, test reports, test procedures, OBD, fuel specifications, HEV testing, and production conformity
- OBD system requirements partially adopted from California CCR Title 13 Section 1968.2 (OBD II); modified monitoring items, thresholds, and monitoring conditions
- Added canister effective volume and initial working capacity test requirements
- Added catalytic converter carrier volume, precious metal content and ratio test requirements
- In-use conformity: added evaporative emission and refueling emission inspection requirements
- Production conformity: added catalytic converter and canister inspection requirements; confirmation checks introduced
- References: EC No 715/2007, EC No 692/2008, ECE R83-07, GTR15 (WLTP), ISO 15031 series, ISO 9141-2, ISO 14230-4

## Technical Details

- Type I test uses WLTP (Worldwide Harmonized Light Vehicles Test Procedure) per GTR15 instead of NEDC
- Type II test changed from idle measurement to RDE (Real Driving Emissions) testing
- PN10 particle number measurement required for RDE testing per Regulation (EU) 2024/1257
- OBD thresholds and monitoring conditions modified from California CCR Title 13 §1968.2
- Durability requirements reflect average expected vehicle lifetime
- Effective date: 2020-07-01 for all sales and registration of light-duty vehicles
