# Emission Regulations Framework

## Definition

Vehicle emission regulations prescribe limits on tailpipe pollutants (NOx, PM, CO, HC, etc.) and
mandate on-board diagnostic (OBD) systems to monitor emission-related components. The framework
is evolving from China 6 (C6) to China 7 (C7), aligned with European EU7 developments.

## Regulatory Landscape

### Chinese Emission Standards

1. **[[gb-18352-6-c6]]** — GB 18352.6-2016, the current China 6 emission standard. Limits and
   measurement methods for light-duty vehicle pollutants. ^[raw/papers/OBD_2_GB+18352.6-2016_C6.md]

2. **[[gb-18352-7-c7]]** — GB 18352.7-XXXX (draft), the upcoming China 7 emission standard.
   Currently in development, referenced by regulatory forecasts from VW Group and CARIAD.
   Includes new RDE (Real Driving Emissions) requirements and stricter pollutant limits.
   ^[raw/papers/OBD_1_GB+18352.7-XXXX_C7_draft.md] ^[raw/articles/QA_12_EU7_Regulation_Forecast_V4.0.md]

3. **[[cn-gb-18285-2018]]** — GB 18285-2018, gasoline vehicle emission limits and measurement
   methods (dual-idle and short driving cycle methods). ^[raw/papers/CN_GB+18285-2018_印刷版.md]

### European/EU Context

4. **[[eu-oj-regulations]]** — EU Official Journal regulations published in 2025, providing
   context for EU7 alignment.

5. **EU7 Regulation Forecast** — The QA_12 document provides the VW Group-wide forecast for
   EU7 regulatory requirements for M1 and N1 vehicles (V4.0, December 2025).

### C7 Premise

The TRRC (Technical Regulatory Roundtable China) has confirmed C7 Emission Premise V6.1 in
June 2026, establishing the groupwide position on C7 emission legislation.
^[raw/articles/Topic 4_TRRC Premise on C7 Emission Legislation V6.1.md] ^[raw/articles/QA_12_EU7_Regulation_Forecast_V4.0.md]

## Key Concepts

- **OBD (On-Board Diagnostics):** Vehicle self-diagnostic system for emission-related components
- **RDE (Real Driving Emissions):** Emission testing under real-world driving conditions
- **WLTP:** Worldwide Harmonized Light Vehicle Test Procedure (lab testing)
- **Pollutant Limits:** NOx, PM, PN (particle number), CO, NMHC, etc.
- **Type Approval:** Process of certifying a vehicle type meets regulatory requirements
- **Same Type Judgment (同一型式判定):** Criteria for determining if vehicle variants share
  the same emission type

## Timeline

- 2016: GB 18352.6 (China 6) published
- 2018: GB 18285 (gasoline vehicle emissions) published
- 2025: EU7 Regulation Forecast V4.0 published
- 2026-Q2: C7 Emission Premise V6.1 confirmed by TRRC
- TBD: GB 18352.7 (China 7) expected implementation
