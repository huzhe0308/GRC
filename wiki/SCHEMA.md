# Wiki Schema

## Domain
Automotive regulations, standards, and technical requirements for the Chinese and international market.
Covers: cybersecurity (CS), functional safety (FuSa), emissions (OBD/EU7/C7), braking/steering (EX),
vehicle network/security requirements (REQ/NET), OTA software updates, ECall, and related regulatory forecasts.

## Conventions
- File names: lowercase, hyphens, no spaces (e.g., `gb-44495-2024.md`)
- Every wiki page starts with YAML frontmatter (see below)
- Use `[[wikilinks]]` to link between pages (minimum 2 outbound links per page)
- When updating a page, always bump the `updated` date
- Every new page must be added to `index.md` under the correct section
- Every action must be appended to `log.md`
- **Provenance markers:** On pages that synthesize 3+ sources, append `^[raw/papers/source-file.md]`
  at the end of paragraphs whose claims come from a specific source. This lets a reader trace each
  claim back without re-reading the whole raw file. Optional on single-source pages where the
  `sources:` frontmatter is enough.
- Raw files in `raw/` are immutable — agent reads but never modifies them.

## Frontmatter
  ```yaml
  ---
  title: Page Title
  created: YYYY-MM-DD
  updated: YYYY-MM-DD
  type: entity | concept | comparison | query | summary
  tags: [from taxonomy below]
  sources: [raw/papers/source-name.md]
  # Optional quality signals:
  confidence: high | medium | low        # how well-supported the claims are
  contested: true                        # set when the page has unresolved contradictions
  contradictions: [other-page-slug]      # pages this one conflicts with
  ---
  ```

`confidence` and `contested` are optional but recommended for opinion-heavy or fast-moving
topics. Lint surfaces `contested: true` and `confidence: low` pages for review so weak claims
don't silently harden into accepted wiki fact.

### raw/ Frontmatter

Raw sources ALSO get a small frontmatter block so re-ingests can detect drift:

```yaml
---
source_url: https://example.com/article   # original URL, if applicable
ingested: YYYY-MM-DD
sha256: <hex digest of the raw content below the frontmatter>
---
```

The `sha256:` lets a future re-ingest of the same URL skip processing when content is unchanged,
and flag drift when it has changed. Compute over the body only (everything after the closing
`---`), not the frontmatter itself.

## Tag Taxonomy

Tags below were generated by reading all 110 raw source files and analyzing their actual content.
Every tag must come from this taxonomy. If a new tag is needed, add it here first, then use it.

### Domain Tags (18)
- cybersecurity — Vehicle cybersecurity engineering, TARA, CSMS, threat analysis, diagnostic security (GB 44495, ISO/SAE 21434, UN R155)
- functional-safety — ISO 26262 / GB/T 34590 functional safety lifecycle: vocabulary, management, concept, system, hardware, software, SOTIF, ASIL, HARA
- emissions — Vehicle emission regulations: pollutant limits, RDE testing, OBD requirements, China 6/7, EU7
- braking — Braking system regulations: service brakes, secondary brakes, parking brakes, ABS, ESC, AEBS (UN R13, GB 21670, GB 12676, UN R152, UN R131)
- steering — Steering system requirements, terminology, functional safety of EPS, automated steering (GB 17675, UN R79)
- adas — Advanced driver assistance and automated driving: AEBS, ALKS, lane keeping, parking automation, DSSAD, driver visibility
- ota — Over-the-air software update regulations: software update management, update process security (GB 44496)
- ev-safety — Electric vehicle safety: REESS/battery safety, electrical safety, high-voltage protection, charging infrastructure (GB 18384)
- ecall — Emergency call systems: eCall MSD, ASN.1 encoding, SASO 2944, UAE S5019, pan-European eCall
- data-security — Data security, privacy protection, data governance, personal information handling, data recording (GB/T 44464)
- connected-vehicle — Internet of Vehicles (IoV) platform security, V2X communication, intelligent vehicle infrastructure, networking
- csms — Cyber Security Management System certification, audit processes, CSMS compliance
- vehicle-testing — Vehicle testing methodologies: simulation, closed-course, field testing, EMC, type approval, same-type judgment
- automated-driving — Automated driving system (ADS) safety requirements: L3/L4 driving automation, dynamic driving tasks, ALKS
- driver-visibility — Driver forward visibility, camera-monitor systems (CMS), head-up displays
- power-supply — Electrical power supply systems for vehicles, power supply architecture, energy management
- diagnostic-security — Secured diagnostic access, UDS service protection (Service 27/29), diagnostic interface security
- regulatory-compliance — Operational procedures, implementation guidelines, regulation forecasts, draft standards

### Source Type Tags (10)
- gb-standard — Chinese mandatory National Standard (GB) issued by SAMR
- gbt-standard — Chinese recommended National Standard (GB/T), typically technical methods and guidelines
- gbz-guideline — Chinese national standardization guiding technical document (GB/Z), non-mandatory
- un-regulation — UN Economic Commission for Europe (UNECE) regulation with supplements
- iso-standard — International Organization for Standardization standard, often adopted as GB/T equivalent
- eu-regulation — European Union regulation or commission implementing regulation
- en-standard — European Standard (EN/CEN) adopted by CEN member countries
- other-national — National standard from other jurisdiction: India (AIS), Korea (KMVSS), Saudi (SASO), UAE (UAE.S)
- internal-spec — Internal company specification, industry recommendation (e.g., VDA), or derived analysis
- draft — Working draft (WD) or 征求意见稿 (draft for comments) of a standard not yet finalized

### Meta Tags (10)
- comparison — Side-by-side analysis, comparison of standards, or mapping between regulations
- timeline — Regulatory timeline, implementation schedule, effective date tracking
- requirement — Specific technical requirements, performance criteria, or mandatory provisions
- test-method — Testing procedures, test conditions, test equipment, verification methods
- glossary — Terminology, vocabulary, abbreviations, and nomenclature definitions
- overview — High-level summary, landscape view, or introduction to a topic area
- procedure — Operational procedure, implementation guideline, or compliance document template
- safety-analysis — Safety analysis methods and procedures: HARA, FMEA, FTA, STPA
- same-type-judgment — Criteria for same-type approval (同一型式判定), vehicle variant equivalency, parameter filing
- presentation — Meeting materials, workshop slides, conference presentation, concept catalog

## Page Thresholds
- **Create a page** when an entity/concept appears in 2+ sources OR is central to one source
- **Add to existing page** when a source mentions something already covered
- **DON'T create a page** for passing mentions, minor details, or things outside the domain
- **Split a page** when it exceeds ~200 lines — break into sub-topics with cross-links
- **Archive a page** when its content is fully superseded — move to `_archive/`, remove from index

## Entity Pages
One page per notable standard, regulation, or specification. Include:
- Standard number and title (Chinese + English)
- Scope and applicability
- Key requirements summary
- Effective dates and implementation timeline
- Relationships to other standards ([[wikilinks]])
- Source references

## Concept Pages
One page per concept or topic. Include:
- Definition / explanation
- Current state of knowledge
- Open questions or debates
- Related concepts ([[wikilinks]])

## Comparison Pages
Side-by-side analyses. Include:
- What is being compared and why
- Dimensions of comparison (table format preferred)
- Verdict or synthesis
- Sources

## Update Policy
When new information conflicts with existing content:
1. Check the dates — newer sources generally supersede older ones
2. If genuinely contradictory, note both positions with dates and sources
3. Mark the contradiction in frontmatter: `contradictions: [page-name]`
4. Flag for user review in the lint report
