CHAPTER 1 GENERAL PROVISIONS
 
Article 1 (Purpose) The purpose of this Notification is to prescribe the matters delegated concerning 
the automotive cyber security management system (CSMS) certification pursuant to Articles 30-9 
through 30-12 of the Motor Vehicle Management Act and Articles 40-26 through 40-30 of the 
Enforcement Regulations of the same Act; matters delegated concerning automotive cyber 
attacks/threats, security measures, etc. pursuant to Article 18-4 of the Regulations for Performance 
and Safety Standards of Motor Vehicle and Vehicle Parts (hereinafter the “Motor Vehicle 
Regulations”); and other detailed matters necessary for the certification of Automotive CSMS. 
 
Article 2 (Definitions) The terms used in this Notification are defined as follows. 
1. “Automotive Cyber Security Management System (CSMS) certification” refers to the process 
of certifying that a series of measures and activities for automotive cyber security of a person 
seeking to obtain an Automotive CSMS certification (hereinafter the “applicant”) pursuant to 
Article 30-9 Paragraph 1 of the Motor Vehicle Management Act (hereinafter the “Act”) comply 
with the certification criteria.
2. “Certification audit” refers to the process of verifying, through a document audit and an on-site 
audit, whether the Automotive CSMS established by the applicant complies with the 
certification criteria.
3. “Document audit” refers to the certification audit conducted through written and face-to-face 
inquiries, etc. to verify whether the documents related to the Automotive CSMS submitted by 
the applicant comply with the certification criteria. 
4. “On-site audit” refers to the certification audit conducted through interviews, on-site checks, etc. 
to verify whether the results of the document audit align with the actual conditions at the site 
and to examine matters that are difficult to assess through a document audit alone. 
 
CHAPTER 2 APPLICATION FOR CERTIFICATION AUDIT AND PAYMENT OF FEES

Criteria for Automotive Cyber Security Management System Certification (Article 6) 
 
Certification Criteria
1 
Scope of Application Motor vehicle manufacturers, etc. shall apply the Automotive CSMS to the 
development, production, and post-production phases of motor vehicles. 
2 
Cyber SecurityManagementProcesses
Motor vehicle manufacturers, etc. shall establish the processes to manage 
automotive cyber security.
3 
Risk IdentificationProcesses
Motor vehicle manufacturers, etc. shall establish the processes for identifying 
automotive cyber security risks.
4 
Risk TreatmentProcesses
Motor vehicle manufacturers, etc. shall establish the processes for assessing and 
treating the identified automotive cyber security risks. 
5 
Risk ManagementVerification Processes
Motor vehicle manufacturers, etc. shall establish the processes to verify that the 
identified automotive cyber security risks are appropriately managed. 
6 
Testing Processes
Motor vehicle manufacturers, etc. shall establish the processes for testing 
automotive cyber security.
7 
Risk AssessmentUpdate Processes
Motor vehicle manufacturers, etc. shall establish the updating processes for 
ensuring that the automotive cyber security risk assessment is kept current. 
8 
Monitoring Processes Motor vehicle manufacturers, etc. shall establish the processes to monitor and 
respond to automotive cyber attacks/threats and vulnerabilities. 
9 
Data CollectionProcesses
Motor vehicle manufacturers, etc. shall establish the processes to collect data on 
attempted automotive cyber attacks/threats. 
10
