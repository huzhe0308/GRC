The requirements for personal information and important data and corresponding test methods

(1) Before the inspection, the enterprise under review shall prepare all system documents and 
template forms necessary for the review of the vehicle data security management system. 
(2) During the inspection, the enterprise under review shall provide the process record documents 
or other supporting materials for the operation of the system. 
3.2. Inspection of requirements for vehicle data security management system 
3.2.1.Inspection of requirements for system integrity(1) Contents of standard
Vehicle data processors shall establish and implement a vehicle data security management 
system, and take technical measures for the protection of vehicle data security to ensure that vehicle 
data is always under effective protection and in legal use. (4.1.1) 
(2) Operation processCheck all materials provided by the enterprise.(3) Determination criteria
To pass the inspection, at least the following conditions shall be met: 
1)
The enterprise has established the vehicle data security management system; 
2) Vehicle data security management system documents are officially released for 
implementation;
3) The enterprise has developed and implemented the specifications for implementation of 
technical measures for vehicle data security protection; 
4) For the requirements in 4.1.2 to 4.1.10 of the standard text, the records regarding their 
implementation shall be provided and shall be acceptable.

Vehicle data processors shall develop vehicle data security objectives and policies, analyze the 
internal and external environment of the vehicle data security management system, and determine the 
boundaries and scope of application of the vehicle data security management system. (4.1.2)

5.2. Inspection of general requirements for personal information processing 
5.2.1.Default non-collection(1) Contents of standard
Vehicle data processors shall process personal information for a clear and reasonable purpose in 
a manner that has the least impact on the rights and interests of the individuals, and the personal 
information shall be directly related to the purpose of the processing. Unless otherwise set by the 
driver, the vehicle shall be set to not collect personal information by default. Personal information 
shall not be provided to the outside of the vehicle in principle unless the consent of the personal 
information subject is obtained. (5.1.1) 
(2) Determination criteria
Check the list of functions and the parameter of data collection equipment to determine one by 
one whether the functions meet the requirements of 5.1.1. The inspection must confirm at least the 
following information before it can be deemed acceptable: 
-
The functions for processing personal information are reflected in the description document; 
-
The description document clearly outlines the specific purpose for processing personal 
information;-
The data processed by each function shall clearly relate to its intended purpose. If any 
functions appear unrelated, the enterprise must provide more detailed description document; 
-
Each function has an enabling path and is disabled by default; 
-
If there is a function to transmit personal information outside the vehicle, the prompt 
message stating "The XX function will transmit XX type of personal information outside the vehicle" 
is required.-
If there is a function to transmit personal information outside the vehicle, the function or 
part of the function to transfer personal information outside the vehicle cannot be used normally 
without consent and authorization.5.2.2.Exceptions of individual's consent(1) Original text
Vehicle data processors may process personal information without obtaining the individual's 
consent under the following exceptions:-
for functions that are necessary to protect the life, health and property safety of natural 
persons in an emergency;-
processing of personal information that is disclosed by individuals themselves or that has 
been legally disclosed within a reasonable scope; 
-
collection of exterior personal information without the individual's consent to ensure the 
driving safety;-
other cases in line with laws, administrative regulations, mandatory national standards and 
other provisions.
