Highlights of Key FuSa/SOTIF standards
Functional safety requirements and verification methods
GB XXXXX-XXXX
• In pre-study (early phase) and expected to issue the plan for official drafting in 2024.
• A mandatory standard in functional safety area for road vehicles.
• It will provide a complete method to verify functional safety in type approval.
Road vehicles - Functional safety
GB/T 34590-2022
• Published in the end of 2022.
• A recommended standard with reference to ISO 26262, not mandatory.
• It is already required by several mandatory standards or standards forecasted as Premise/Risk Radar. Only the
functional safety requirements listed in other mandatory standards shall be met.
Road vehicles - Safety of the intended functionality
GB/T 43267-2023
• Published in the end of 2023.
• A recommended standard with reference to ISO 21448, not mandatory.
• It is expected to be required by other standards in the future. Only the SOTIF requirements listed in other
mandatory standards shall be met.
